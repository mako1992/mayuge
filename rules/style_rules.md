# Style Rules（往復の中で生まれたルール集）

ChatGPT ⇄ Claude の往復で指摘されたことを、**1 ルール 1 行**で追記していく。
このファイルを毎回プロンプトに添付することで、次回以降の往復をなくす。

## Voice & Tone
- First-person, warm, conversational — like writing to a smart friend in the industry.
- No jargon or business-speak. No "In today's world..." / "In this fast-paced..." openings.
- Avoid absolute claims ("always", "never", "the best"). Prefer "in my experience", "what I'm seeing".

## Structure
- One clear takeaway per issue.
- 800–1,200 words (≈5-minute read).
- Exactly one personal anecdote, with a concrete detail (place, product, price, date).
- End with one reply-worthy question.

## Japan-specific
- Give product names in English first, then romaji/Japanese in parentheses on first mention.
- Convert prices: ¥ first, then approx. USD (state the rate or "approx.").
- Explain Japanese retail terms briefly the first time (e.g., konbini, depachika).

## Facts & Credibility
- Every number needs a source or "observed in store on [date]".
- Never invent quotes, statistics, or company statements.

## Paywall
- Free section must deliver real value on its own; paid section goes deeper (analysis, sourcing, implications).
