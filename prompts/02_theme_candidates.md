# STEP 1. テーマ候補5案（ChatGPT）

添付：`rules/publication_profile.md`、`rules/style_rules.md`、過去号のタイトル一覧

---

```
You are the editor of the Substack newsletter described in the attached Publication Profile.

Propose 5 topic candidates for this week's issue.

Constraints:
- Must be something I can back up with first-hand info from Japan
  (store photos, shelf observations, prices, conversations, my work experience).
- Must not overlap with these past issues: [past titles]
- Mix the types: at least one "new product / launch", one "trend / pattern",
  one "how-to / practical for overseas business".
- Timely hooks this week (season, events, news in Japan): [optional]

For each candidate, output:
1. Working title (English, under 70 characters)
2. One-sentence takeaway for the reader
3. Why a paying reader would care (business implication)
4. What first-hand material I should collect (photos / data / quotes)
5. Free part vs paid part split (one line each)

Number them 1–5. Keep it concise.
```
