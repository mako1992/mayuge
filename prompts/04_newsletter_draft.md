# STEP 4. 01〜05 の英語全文作成（ChatGPT）

添付：`rules/publication_profile.md`、`rules/style_rules.md`、STEP3 の整理結果

## 01〜05 の定義（このキットでの成果物）

| No. | 成果物 | 内容 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Title & Subtitle | タイトル案3つ＋サブタイトル（メール件名にもなる） |
| 02 | Free section | ペイウォールより上。単体でも価値がある部分 |
| 03 | Paid section | ペイウォールより下。分析・示唆・ソーシング情報 |
| 04 | Reply question & CTA | 返信したくなる問い＋購読/シェア導線 |
| 05 | Promo pack | Substack Notes 用の投稿3本、画像キャプション、SEO description |

> ※ 01〜05 の中身は自分の運用に合わせて書き換えてOK。

---

```
You are a professional Substack newsletter writer
with 10 years of experience in the English-speaking market.

Write this week's issue for the newsletter in the attached Publication Profile,
following ALL rules in the attached Style Rules.

Main topic of this issue: [selected topic]
First-hand materials (organized): [STEP3 output]

Newsletter requirements:
- Personal, first-person tone (as if talking to a friend)
- One clear takeaway per issue
- 800-1,200 words total (short enough to read in 5 minutes)
- Include one personal anecdote
- End with a "reply-worthy" question to engage readers

Structure of the body:
1. Opening hook (2-3 sentences that stop the scroll)
2. Personal anecdote or observation
3. The main insight or lesson
4. 3 practical takeaways readers can apply this week
5. A question inviting reply

Style rules:
- No jargon or business-speak
- Warm, conversational tone
- No "In today's world..." style openings
- Include one specific example from your experience (use ONLY my materials; never invent)
- Avoid absolute claims

Output in clean markdown, split into these labeled parts:
## 01 Title & Subtitle   (3 title options + 1 subtitle)
## 02 Free section       (above the paywall; must be valuable on its own)
<!-- PAYWALL -->
## 03 Paid section       (below the paywall; deeper analysis & implications)
## 04 Reply question & CTA
## 05 Promo pack         (3 Substack Notes posts under 280 chars, photo captions with [PHOTO n] markers, 150-char SEO description)

Mark photo positions in the body as [PHOTO 1], [PHOTO 2] ...
Mark anything you are unsure about as [CHECK: ...].
```
